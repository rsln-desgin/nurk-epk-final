# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Promoters, booking agents, festival talent buyers, label A&R. They arrive from a direct link, open the page in a browser on desktop or phone, and need to make a fast confidence decision: "Is this act worth the risk?" They are not fans — they are professionals evaluating a business proposition. They scan fast, look for social proof and professionalism, and want to contact immediately if they like what they see.

## Product Purpose

A single-page Electronic Press Kit for NURK (Uku Nurk), a Tallinn-based DJ and producer. The EPK is the primary booking asset — a trust document that backs up an outreach conversation. Success looks like a promoter landing on the page and sending a booking inquiry within 2 minutes, or forwarding the link to a decision-maker with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Cold, precise, credible. Not hype — earned. Three words: sharp, composed, real.

Voice is direct and economical. No press-release purple prose, no AI-inflated superlatives. The copy says facts; the design does the emoting.

## Anti-references

- Generic club/rave poster aesthetic (neon on black, gig-listing typography)
- SaaS landing page patterns (hero metrics, feature grids, testimonial carousels)
- Over-produced DJ bio sites that feel more like a film promo than a working artist
- TikTok-brained social profiles with engagement-bait formatting

## Design Principles

1. **Credentials over claims.** Show the venues, the crowds, the releases. Let facts speak. Remove any copy that doesn't add a specific, verifiable detail.
2. **Professionals read fast.** Every section should pass a 5-second scan. Lead with the most important fact; supporting detail follows.
3. **Quiet confidence.** The page doesn't sell hard. It presents clearly and lets the work close the deal.
4. **One action per section.** Each screen has one obvious next step. Don't compete for attention within a section.
5. **System coherence over decoration.** The glass + ice-cyan system is the brand. New additions extend it; they don't accent it with unrelated elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already wired). All interactive elements need visible focus states. Text on glass surfaces must maintain 4.5:1 contrast at all usage sizes.
